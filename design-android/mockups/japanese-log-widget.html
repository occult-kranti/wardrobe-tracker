System surfaces, one concept sheet — not an app screen; the bar is shown only to keep the family resemblance with Today. Above, the 4×2 home-screen widget in its unlogged state: Same as Tuesday logs the day in one tap without opening the app — the 2-tap becomes 1-tap from the launcher — with the widget's other two states (matured plan, logged) listed beneath; it sits there and never buzzes. The launcher rounds the shell with its own radius; everything inside stays radius-2. Below, the shade: the Log a wear quick-settings tile the owner hangs from the tile editor, and the quick-log card it opens over any app, where a matured plan is a dressed hanger carrying its paper tag in the second metal — Wore it lifts the tag, It didn't happen returns the hanger bare, and the record moves only when the person does. Pull-based always; the app never requests notification permission.
