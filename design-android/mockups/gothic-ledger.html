The Ledger — the tailor's counter book, one room of the house behind More. Category share sits in a rose window: rims and spokes at structure weight, one lancet petal per light in the two alternating metals, a quatrefoil eye at the hub — no glass, no colour fill; the share is read by angle and counted in the figures beside it, never by colour alone. Below, the manuscript leaf rules cost-per-wear in plain columns — the Kolhapuris at ₹31 a wear, the silk kurta at ₹2,967 and three days out — and jewellery sits out rather than speak from too few wears. Utilization is a bar and two figures; ₹64,300 rests and is said once, like a balance. Tapping any row opens that piece's sheet; the dial is read-only. No art moment lives here — numbers in this house hold still, and nothing celebrates, badges, or streaks.
