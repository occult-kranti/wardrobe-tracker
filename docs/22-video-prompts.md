## PROMPT 1 — The demo video (60–90s, product-led)

---

Create a calm, premium product demo video for **Toile** — a private,
local-first wardrobe tracking web app. Positioning in one line: *your
wardrobe, on record* — it tracks what you own, what you actually wear, and
what each piece costs per wear, entirely on your device: no account, no
cloud, no subscription, nothing for sale inside.

**Links to show on screen (and only these):**
- App (glass edition, primary): `occult-kranti.github.io/wardrobe-tracker/v2/`
- App (paper edition): `occult-kranti.github.io/wardrobe-tracker`
URLs appear only twice: small lower-third near the end, and on the final card.

**Look:** letterpress elegance on dark "obsidian" glass — near-black surfaces
(#0A0B0F ground), translucent frosted-glass cards, rose-gold hairline borders
(#D39A85), a warm rose-gold light that drifts across the glass, serif
display type (Georgia-class), tiny uppercase monospaced ledger labels,
hand-drawn line artwork of wardrobes/hangers/keepsakes in the margins (never
human figures — the brand draws furniture and garments, never bodies). A
carmine red (#BE1231) exists ONLY as a wax-seal accent, never as alarm color.

**Pacing (strict):** slow, deliberate, launch-film canon. One idea per scene.
The product is on screen within the first two seconds — no logo intro. Each
scene: a single action at real speed, then the result HOLDS in stillness for
2–3 seconds. When a caption appears, the picture freezes beneath it; captions
are one line, at most eight words, uppercase serif in a small dark chip,
centered; they live ~2 seconds and leave before motion resumes. Total 8–9
scenes, 60–90 seconds.

**The flow (scene by scene):**
1. Open on a full closet grid under glass — photographs and hand-drawn
   garment sketches side by side, warm light moving across the cards.
   Caption: "Your wardrobe, on record."
2. A fresh start: an empty wardrobe is created with just a name — no email,
   no password. Caption: "It begins empty, on this device."
3. One garment is added with a form (name, category chip, cost) and appears
   in the grid as a drawn sketch. Caption: "The first piece enters the record."
4. The core loop: today's wear is logged in two taps — button, pick the
   piece, done; the day's entry appears. This plays at REAL speed, never
   compressed. Caption: "The day, answered in two taps."
5. Analytics on a year-deep closet: cost-per-wear figures, utilization, a
   quiet line reading how much money is "resting" in unworn pieces — stated
   once like a bank balance, no zoom, no siren. Caption: "Cost per wear,
   never paywalled."
6. A wish-listed coat waits out a 7-day cooling-off timer; a released entry
   reads "$210 stayed yours." Caption: "Seven days of silence, then one question."
7. A calendar where future days are visibly *plans*, not wears; today asks
   "You had this down for today — Wore it / It didn't happen," and only the
   tap moves the record. Caption: "The record never assumes a wear."
8. The same closet re-dressed through two more themed "rooms" (a warm
   rose-gold room, an ivory paper room), then returning home to the dark
   glass. Caption once at the first change: "Six rooms, one record."
9. End card: the word TOILE in wide-tracked serif, a short carmine rule
   beneath it, then the two URLs. Caption: "No account, no cloud, no
   subscription." Hold 4 seconds. Nothing else.

**Audio:** NO voice-over. A sparse ambient score: soft synth pad around 60
BPM felt-tempo, occasional windchime/struck-glass notes in a minor pentatonic,
one gentle swell under scene 8, near-silence and a single decaying chime over
the end card. Optionally one soft felt "tick" sound on the two log taps.

**Never:** exclamation points, urgency copy ("now", "don't miss"), badges,
streaks, confetti, progress meters, shame framing of unworn clothes (they are
"resting", never "wasted"), shop links or prices as temptation, human bodies
in illustrations, red as an alert color, fake speed on the logging
interaction, any implication of cloud sync.

## PROMPT 2 — The ad video (30s spot, 16:9 + 9:16 variants)

---

Create a 30-second ad for **Toile** (`occult-kranti.github.io/wardrobe-tracker/v2/`)
— a private wardrobe ledger web app. The ad's single idea: **every other
wardrobe app wants to sell you something; this one keeps a record for you.**

**Structure:**
- 0–3s HOOK: extreme close-up of a dark glass interface where a warm
  rose-gold light drifts across a grid of clothing cards. On-screen line:
  "Your closet already knows what it costs."
- 3–10s THE TURN: three quick-but-calm beats (2s each, real UI): a wear
  logged in two taps · a figure reading "$0.56/wear" under a favorite shirt ·
  a wishlist card reading "$210 stayed yours."
- 10–20s THE PROOF: one unbroken 10-second shot, slow scroll down a
  year-deep closet — photographs beside hand-drawn garment sketches, tiny
  ledger captions under each piece. On-screen line at 14s: "Worn is a number
  here, not a feeling."
- 20–26s THE DIFFERENCE: the same closet melts through three themed rooms
  (dark glass → rose-gold → ivory paper). On-screen line: "No account. No
  cloud. Nothing for sale."
- 26–30s CARD: TOILE wordmark, carmine underline, URL
  `occult-kranti.github.io/wardrobe-tracker/v2/`, silence except one chime.

**Tone:** museum-quiet confidence. The ad never raises its voice — the
category is loud commerce apps, and stillness IS the differentiation. Serif
type, uppercase micro-labels, dark glass aesthetic with rose-gold hairlines.
No people on camera, no hands, no bodies in artwork — the interface and the
drawn line-art (hangers, a dress form, a strand of pearls) are the only cast.

**Audio:** ambient pad + windchime score only; a single soft tick on each of
the two logging taps; the final chime decays into silence over the card. No
voice-over — if the platform demands VO, use a single calm sentence at 20s:
"Toile. Your wardrobe, on record." — nothing else.

**Deliver:** one 16:9 master (1920×1080) and one native 9:16 re-edit
(1080×1920, captions in the top third, first frame already in motion).

**Never:** exclamation points, discount/urgency framing, gamification
imagery, shame about unworn clothes, red as alarm, fake UI, cloud-sync
implication, bodies.

## Notes for whoever runs these

- The app really exists at both URLs; the v2 glass build is the one that
  photographs best. All UI shown in the prompts is real, shipped behavior.
- The six rooms are: pattern room (ivory paper), salon, gilding room
  (rose+gold), dye house (dark plum), obsidian (black glass, rose-gold
  hairlines), atelier (dark). Ads should stay in obsidian and visit at most
  two others.
- Sample data: three personas (Aarav, Vikram, Meher) ship in the app under
  "Add the sample wardrobes" — a year of honest wear history each, so any
  screen recording lands on real-looking numbers.
- The voice rules above are the product's actual contract (docs/05, docs/06);
  a video that breaks them misrepresents the product.
