Opening a wardrobe. Up to 900ms: the doors rotate outward on their hinge edges into the row's gap — never over the name — the almirah's padlock unlatches and swings on its hasp, and a strung tag with the wardrobe's initials ties itself to the rail inside. Navigation waits for the moment to finish; if the local read runs long the doors hold ajar with a ruled writing line inside. Reduce Motion shows the open-door still, then proceeds.
