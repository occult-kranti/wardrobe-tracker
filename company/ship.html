Shipping Almari

The launch plan — what shipped this sprint, what is left and in what order, what we depend on, and the gates we will not lower.

The Workroom · The Tech Workbench · The launch plan · The app

The Workroom holds the company and the Tech Workbench holds the tasks. This page holds the plan of record for getting Almari from a mobile-first web app to a native app in testers' hands. It is a plan, not marketing: where it and the boards disagree, the boards win and this page gets fixed.

Phasing is in weeks from the start of the sprint, never calendar dates. A date would be a promise we cannot keep; a week number is a sequence we can.

Where we are

This sprint · shipped

The sprint took Almari from a desktop-strong PWA to a mobile-first build with a feed that earns its name. Five pieces of it are already on the live site; one is still in flight.

The living feed

A deterministic persona engine derives dated look-posts from each persona's own outfits, events and seasons, merged idempotently at boot, capped, tombstone-aware — and it never touches a real user's posts. Cards are image-first 4:5 in a hairline plate, with author, date, caption and scope. The only engagement mechanic is a private, on-device save: no counts, no ranking, nothing public.

Feed & sync correctness

Sub-day timestamps ended the same-day scramble. Cross-tab writes merge per entity instead of last-blob-wins. Taken-down posts stay down, because tombstones outlive reseeds. Accepting a borrow request in Chats now creates the real loan in the Rail ledger — one system, not two half-wired ones. 72 new feed checks stand watch over all of it.

The mobile-first pass

A 16px floor on every text input kills the iOS auto-zoom. Modals become bottom sheets on small screens, with keyboard safety. The More sheet dismisses like a sheet — backdrop, outside tap, Esc. The calendar is a snap strip instead of a deep scroll, overscroll is contained, and the home screen gets the real mark: PNG icons and an apple-touch-icon instead of a screenshot.

First-run and the empty rooms

A four-beat first-run tutorial — add a piece, the two-tap log, Before You Buy, the export reminder — dismissible forever, never blocking, replayable from Settings. Every empty state was audited to teach one thing, and the copy law held: the one exclamation point stays unspent.

In flight this wave

Sign-in with optional Supabase sync — the device stays the default home of every wardrobe; an account is optional and sync is opt-in per wardrobe — and the AI photo-intake proxy layer. Neither is claimed as shipped until the suites say so.

The new track

Owner direction, added this sprint. This is the road from the web app to an app in testers' hands.

1Sign-in + opt-in cloud syncWeek 3–4In flight

Supabase auth and Postgres. The device remains the default — sign-in is optional, sync is switched on per wardrobe, and a wardrobe that never touches an account is a first-class wardrobe, forever.

2AI photo intake, via a proxyWeek 3–4In flight

Photograph the closet; a vision model drafts the catalogue; nothing is written until you say so. Claude Sonnet (Anthropic) sits behind a Supabase edge function that holds the key server-side — the app never sees it. The relay routes by model name, so Kimi K3 remains one constant away as the fallback. Live-verified on the owner's own camera roll: 16 pieces read from 5 photographs, every crop a real JPEG. Also shipped: feed import — drop a screenshot of an Instagram grid and the solo posts are read into cropped garment drafts, while group photos are left alone.

3Badges & rewardsIn design

Positive-only and fashion-based: marks for wearing what you own, never pressure, never shame, never a streak that punishes a missed day. This amends the old no-gamification rule deliberately, and the amendment is written into the non-negotiables below.

4The Expo appAfter the web gatesPlanned

Expo + React Native, one codebase for iOS and Android, with EAS builds and over-the-air updates. The PWA stays the free web channel.

5The alphaTwo weeksPlanned

20–50 testers, two weeks, distributed by QR code — Expo Go or EAS Update, so a tester joins by pointing a camera at a square. No store publish for the alpha.

The gates

Alpha exit criteria · pre-registered

From docs/28-the-company.md §4.4, absolute and pre-registered. Measurement stays manual — moderated sessions, diary studies, volunteered exports — because the app has no telemetry and never will.

Gate 1 · Speed≤ 2 tapsMedian assisted logging flow, stopwatched in moderated sessions.
Gate 2 · Week 1≥ 60%of diary-study participants complete the first week.
Gate 3 · Week 12≥ 40%of the alpha cohort logs at least one wear in week twelve.
Gate 4 · Suites390pxEvery suite green at phone width — build, brand, migrate, demo, feed, cast, intake, flows, features.
The ruleIf a gate fails, the alpha does not graduate. The numbers do not move to meet a date; the date moves to meet the numbers.

What we will not do

The non-negotiables · amended by owner direction

Two of these changed this sprint, on purpose and in the open — local-first became the default rather than the only mode, and the blanket ban on gamification became a ban on everything except positive, fashion-based marks. The rest are untouched.

  1. Local-first remains the default.Amended

    Sign-in is optional; sync is opt-in per wardrobe; the app on one device, with no account, is the product — not a trial of the product.

  2. No commerce.

    No shop links, affiliate codes, or retailer suggestions. A feature that talks you out of buying cannot profit from buying.

  3. No shame mechanics.

    No guilt screens, no red alarm colours on low-wear pieces, no wasted-money framing. An app users lie to is worse than no app.

  4. Badges and rewards are positive-only and fashion-based.Amended

    Marks for the behaviour the product exists to create — wearing what you own. Never a streak that punishes a missed day, never confetti for spending.

  5. No gendered anything.

    The app asks what you own, never who you are.

  6. Lossless export, permanently.

    Every field, including ones added by future versions. The record is never the hostage.
